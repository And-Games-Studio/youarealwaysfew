Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

In short: You Are Always Few is free to play on Android. Its ads are videos you choose to watch, shown through Google AdMob, never in the middle of play. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland the game asks for your consent before ads use your personal data, and you can change your choice at any time in Options. There are no accounts of our own: your progress stays on your phone, and if you sign in to Google Play Games, a copy of it is kept with your Google account so it comes back on a new phone; on an iPhone, Game Center keeps that copy in your iCloud. The game doesn't ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

Who we are

You Are Always Few is made by Andrea Ebano, publishing as And Games, based in Portugal. Andrea Ebano is the controller of the personal data described here. Contact: ebano.andrea.games@gmail.com.

What data the game uses, and why

Playing

Tano's level, skills and Obols, the rest of what the Ledger keeps, and your options are stored on your phone. We don't receive them.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Ads only ever play when you choose to watch a video: to come back once when you fall in a Breach, or to double the Obols a Breach gave you. They are shown by Google AdMob, a Google service. To show and measure them, and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads software in the game sends Google:

  • your phone's advertising ID (you can reset or delete it in your phone's settings, under Google → Ads);
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location (country, region) can be worked out;
  • information about your device and the app: model, operating system and its language, the app's version;
  • how you interact with the ads: which ones were shown, whether you watched or tapped them;
  • your consent choices, where you were asked.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, a consent form from Google appears the first time you open the game. It lists the purposes and the ad technology providers that work with Google; ads are personalised, and information is stored on or read from your phone for ads, only if you agree. If you don't, you can still choose to watch videos, but they aren't based on your personal data. You can change your choice whenever you like in the game's Options. The game asks Google only for ads rated suitable for most audiences. Google uses this data as described in its own policies, including how Google uses information from apps that use its services. We never see your advertising ID or the other data Google receives; we only get totals, such as how many ads were shown.

Google Play Games (optional)

You can sign in to Google Play Games, but you don't have to. If you do, the game receives your Play Games player identifier and display name, and saves a copy of your progress (the same data as on your phone, not your options) in your Google account through Play Games, so you get it back on a new phone. We don't receive it. Google Play Games is a Google service, covered by Google's privacy policy; you can delete the game's saved data, or your whole Play Games profile, in the Google Play Games app.

Game Center and iCloud (optional, on iPhone)

On an iPhone the game uses Game Center instead of Google Play Games, and only if you are signed in to Game Center and to iCloud on that iPhone. Game Center then keeps a copy of your progress (the same data as on your phone, not your options) among its saved games, which Apple stores in your iCloud Drive, so you get it back on a new iPhone. The game doesn't read your Game Center name or identifier, and we don't receive the copy. Game Center and iCloud are Apple services, covered by Apple's privacy policy; you can delete the game's saved data in your iPhone's Settings, under your name, iCloud, Manage Account Storage.

Technical data

Google receives your IP address and basic technical information when the game connects to it, to make the connection work and keep its services secure. The Unity engine, which the game is made with, may also send Unity anonymous information about your device (model, operating system, graphics hardware) when the game starts.

Legal bases

Google Play Games or Game Center sign-in and saved progress, when you choose them, are handled to give you what you asked for (Article 6(1)(b) of the GDPR). Ads keep the game free: personalised ads, and storing or reading information on your phone for ads, rest on your consent (Article 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time in the game's Options; ads that aren't personalised, their measurement and fraud prevention rest on our legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f)), which you can object to in the same place.

How long we keep it

Data on your phone stays until you uninstall the game or clear its data; progress saved in Google Play Games, or in iCloud through Game Center, stays until you delete it there. Your consent choices for ads stay on your phone until you change them or clear the game's data; Google keeps ad data for the periods set out in its privacy policy.

Your rights

You can ask to access or delete your data, to restrict or object to its use, and to receive it in a portable format. Write to ebano.andrea.games@gmail.com; to delete your data, see Delete your data. To withdraw your consent to ads, or object to them, use the game's Options; to stop ads being linked to your phone, reset or delete its advertising ID in the phone's settings. You can also complain to a data protection authority: in Portugal, the CNPD (cnpd.pt), or the one where you live.

Children

You Are Always Few is meant for players aged 13 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from children under 13; if you think a child has used the game in a way that sent us data, write to us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
