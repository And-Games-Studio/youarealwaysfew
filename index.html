You Are Always Few

The Many are endless. You are always few.

Ultimo is the last name in the Tally: alone against endless hordes, he dies, grows and goes back into the Breaches until he becomes the strongest. His level never resets, every kill is counted, and the Tally erases nothing.

Eight Breaches, each with a danger of its own. Thirty-six skills, each with its own way of fighting. Deeds to do, Writs to find and a Ledger that keeps it all. In English and Italian.
