Delete your data

You Are Always Few by And Games has no accounts of its own: your progress lives on your phone, and in your Google account if you signed in to Google Play Games. We don't receive it. Here is how to delete what the game keeps.

On your phone

Uninstall You Are Always Few, or clear its data in your phone's settings (Apps → You Are Always Few → Storage → Clear data). Your progress can't be recovered afterwards.

Want anything else deleted? Email ebano.andrea.games@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data". We reply within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Everything the game keeps on your phone: Ultimo's level, skills and Obols, the rest of the Ledger, your options and your consent choices for ads.

What stays

  • Progress saved in Google Play Games, if you signed in: delete the game's saved data, or your whole Play Games profile, in the Google Play Games app.
  • Ad data held by Google: to stop ads being linked to your phone, reset or delete its advertising ID in the phone's settings (Google → Ads). Google keeps ad data as its privacy policy describes.

Details are in our privacy policy.
